# Super Scholar V3.10 — Venue-conditioned Retrieval & Actionability

## Scope

V3.10 keeps the product boundary at: upload research → understand research → recommend CS publication venues → compare → select. It does not add manuscript rewriting or historical-paper RAG.

## Recommendation changes

1. **Venue-conditioned retrieval** searches the research query inside a bounded set of candidate OpenAlex Sources for recent years. A conference series may resolve to several annual proceedings Sources; side-event Sources such as workshops/companions/tutorials are rejected.
2. **Academic Fit** ranks scholarly suitability. **Current Actionability** is independent and, for conferences, requires a verified open edition/deadline plus passing Required actionability constraints. Closed or unknown editions may remain academic targets but do not enter `当前可投`.
3. Research Match uses the available subset of: venue-conditioned OpenAlex recent works 48%, global OpenAlex direct-publication evidence 14%, CS scope 18%, Semantic Scholar 8%, structured References 8%, Source topics 4%. CCF does not enter content-match scoring.
4. Source-topic histograms are auxiliary only. Unknown is never converted into zero simply to produce a complete score.
5. Structured bibliography matching protects ambiguous acronyms such as FAST/CASE/DATE/CHI/SC from ordinary-word false positives.

## CCF seventh-edition handling

The official seventh-edition catalog has 295 journal placements. Four cross-category placements share stable journal identities, yielding 291 canonical unique journal records. V3.10 validates 295 placements, preserves every placement as provenance, and canonicalizes them to 291 unique journal identities for recommendation.

`ccf = U` means the CCF status cannot currently be verified. `ccf = N` is used only when a complete verified catalog was available and the venue was confirmed absent.

## Reliability rules

- Successful venue-conditioned retrieval with no close works = verified score 0.
- Unresolved Source/series or failed retrieval = Unknown.
- Conference deadlines that are closed/unknown never erase academic fit, but they cannot be presented as currently actionable.
- JCR/JIF/APC/review-speed values remain Unknown unless backed by an integrated verifiable source.
- Research Match / Academic Fit are not acceptance probabilities.

## Validation

The release contains regression tests for CCF placement canonicalization, ambiguous bibliography acronyms, academic-vs-actionability separation, venue-conditioned ranking, conference Source-series filtering, and OpenAlex source/date-filter construction.
